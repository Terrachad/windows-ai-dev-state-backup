# Community expectations

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome users with different technical backgrounds, operating systems and levels of experience. Disagree with an idea without attacking its author.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, credential sharing, deliberate data-loss advice, spam and deceptive promotion are not acceptable. Do not pressure someone to publish private logs, credentials or conversations in order to get help.

Maintainers may edit or remove unsafe content, close disruptive discussions, and restrict participation where necessary to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportional, documented privately where appropriate, and applied consistently.

Report public spam/abuse through GitHub's reporting tools. For project-specific concerns, contact a maintainer through an available private channel. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md rather than public discussion.

Participation does not entitle anyone to a response deadline or free personal recovery services. Respect volunteer time and prioritize reproducible reports and careful review.
